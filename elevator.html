Smart elevator control system

A six-floor car with a position loop around a speed loop. Encoder and load-cell signals are noisy, so a digital filter cleans them before the controller sees them. Press floors, then switch the filter on and off.

Try this

  1. Filter off, noise high, go to floor 5: the force chatters and the ride jerk climbs.
  2. Filter on, cutoff at 1 Hz: the noise is gone but the speed loop lags and the car sways.
  3. Raise the load with the filter off: spikes in the load cell throw off the gravity compensation.
